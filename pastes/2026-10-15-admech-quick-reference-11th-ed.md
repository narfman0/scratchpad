## Core rules

### Turn structure
Each player turn runs: **Command → Movement → Shooting → Charge → Fight**.

- **Command:** both players gain 1CP (so ~2CP per battle round total for you). Then battle-shock rolls for your units that are already battle-shocked or at/below half-strength. Then "in your Command phase" abilities (Doctrina, Cawl's Canticle, Thulia's Icon, Datasmith protocol, Cybernetica stratagems).
- **Movement:** every unit picks one move type: remain stationary, normal, advance, or fall back. Reserves arrive here.
- **Shooting:** each eligible unit shoots once.
- **Charge:** declare and roll 2D6, move into engagement.
- **Fight:** chargers / Fights First go first, then alternate.

### Moves
- **Normal:** up to M. Can't end engaged.
- **Advance:** M + D6. No charging or actions that turn. Only [ASSAULT] weapons can shoot (Conqueror Doctrina gives all ranged weapons Assault).
- **Fall back:** up to M, from engagement. No shooting, charging or actions afterwards unless a rule says so (Ironstriders and Overloaded Safeguards bypass this). A battle-shocked unit has to make a *Desperate Escape*: a hazard roll per model, but it can move through enemies.
- **Charge:** 2D6, targets within 12". Must end engaged with the charge targets.

### Transports (Dunerider)
Units can get out only if the transport didn't Advance or Fall Back that phase.
- **Transport moved:** *rapid disembark*, within 3". The unit can shoot but can't charge.
- **Transport stayed still or hasn't moved yet:** *tactical disembark*, within 3", then the unit makes a normal or advance move itself.
- **Neither works (e.g. blocked in):** *combat disembark*, within 6", with a hazard roll per model. The unit becomes battle-shocked and can't charge.

### Shooting essentials
- **Cover:** the attacker gets **-1 to hit** if every model in the target is either infantry inside a terrain area, or partly blocked from view by terrain. Stealth grants cover; Ignores Cover cancels it.
- **Hidden:** infantry inside light or dense terrain can't be seen by enemies beyond **15"** unless they shot this turn or last turn. On turn 1 nobody counts as having shot. Lords of the Forge's Data Screed can keep a Tech-Priest's unit hidden after shooting (roll 2+).
- **Lone Operative:** can't be targeted from more than 12" away unless attached to a unit. Cawl and Thulia get it while within 3" of another AdMech unit.
- **Heavy:** +1 to hit if the unit moved no more than 3", isn't engaged, and wasn't set up this turn.
- **Snap shooting (Overwatch):** hits only on unmodified 6s, no re-rolls, max 24", one target. Torrent weapons still auto-hit.

### Objectives and battle-shock
- **Control:** at the end of each phase and turn, total the OC within range of each objective. Higher total controls it.
- **Secured objectives:** stay yours with nobody on them until the opponent has more OC there at the end of a phase. Rangers' *Objective Scouted* does this.
- **Battle-shocked units:** OC becomes "–", you can't use stratagems on them, and they can't do actions. Test in your Command phase on 2D6 against Ld.

### Actions
A unit doing an action can't shoot or charge that turn, unless a rule allows it (Battleclade, Holy Avarice). Moving cancels the action. Units can't start one if they Advanced, Fell Back, are engaged or are battle-shocked.

### Weapon keywords (short form)
- **Anti-X Y+:** wound rolls of Y+ against keyword X are criticals.
- **Assault:** can shoot after Advancing.
- **Blast:** +1 attack per 5 models in the target.
- **Devastating Wounds:** critical wounds become mortal wounds equal to D. No saves.
- **Extra Attacks:** used in addition to the model's other melee weapon.
- **Hazardous:** after attacking, one hazard roll per hazardous weapon used.
- **Heavy:** see above.
- **Ignores Cover:** target gets no cover.
- **Lethal Hits:** critical hits (6s) auto-wound.
- **Melta X:** +X damage at half range or closer.
- **Pistol / Close-Quarters:** can shoot while engaged.
- **Precision:** can allocate to a visible Character in the unit.
- **Rapid Fire X:** +X attacks at half range or closer.
- **Sustained Hits X:** critical hits score X extra hits.
- **Torrent:** auto-hits.
- **Twin-linked:** re-roll wound rolls.

### Unit abilities (short form)
- **Deadly Demise X:** when destroyed, on a 6 each unit within 6" takes X mortal wounds.
- **Deep Strike:** arrive from reserves anywhere more than 8" from enemies.
- **Feel No Pain X+:** roll for each wound lost; X+ ignores it.
- **Firing Deck X:** X embarked models can shoot through the transport.
- **Infiltrators:** deploy anywhere more than 8" from enemy units and the enemy deployment zone.
- **Leader / Support:** attaches to a bodyguard unit. A unit can take one Leader plus one Support.
- **Mobile:** move through dense terrain horizontally.
- **Scouts X":** pre-game move of up to X".
- **Stealth:** always counts as in cover against shooting.

### Core stratagems
- **Command Re-roll (1CP):** re-roll one hit, wound, damage, save, Advance, charge or attack-count roll.
- **Fire Overwatch (1CP):** at the end of the enemy's Movement phase, snap-shoot at one enemy within 24".
- **Smokescreen (1CP):** at the start of the enemy's Shooting phase, a SMOKE unit (and anything partly hidden behind it) gets cover.
- **Heroic Intervention (1CP; +1CP option):** at the end of the enemy's Charge phase, charge a unit that just charged. The +1CP option lets you hit any enemy within 6", with the charge roll capped at 6.
- **Counteroffensive (2CP):** after an enemy unit fights, your unit fights next.
- **Rapid Ingress (1CP):** a reserve unit arrives at the end of the enemy's Movement phase. Not usable in battle round 1.
- **Insane Bravery (1CP, once per battle):** auto-pass a battle-shock roll.
- **Explosives / Grenades (1CP):** a GRENADES unit rolls 6D6 against an enemy within 8"; each 4+ is a mortal wound. Only Skystalkers have Grenades in this army.
- **Crushing Impact / Tank Shock (1CP):** after a monster or vehicle charges, roll D6 equal to its Toughness. Each 5+ is a mortal wound to the enemy (max 6); each 1 is a mortal wound to you.
- **Epic Challenge (1CP):** a Character's melee attacks get Precision.

## Army rules

### Doctrina Imperatives
Pick one each battle round. It applies to the whole army.
- **Protector:** +1 BS, and ranged weapons gain [HEAVY]. Battleline units, and units within 6" of Battleline, are also hit at **-1 in melee**. Use it on turn 1, when shooting from cover, and when you're about to be charged.
- **Conqueror:** +1 WS, and ranged weapons gain [ASSAULT]. Battleline units, and units within 6" of Battleline, also get **+1 AP** on all attacks. Use it on the turns you attack.
- **Both at once:** Thulia's *Fires of Mars* (one Skitarii unit) and Cybernetica's *Transcendent Cogitation* (one vehicle) turn on both for one unit.
- **Battleline in your list:** Rangers, Vanguard, and Cawl (while his *Mantra of Discipline* Canticle is active).

## Detachments

### Lords of the Forge (2 DP)
- **War-Form Mantles:** every Tech-Priest has a **4+ invulnerable save** and **5+ Feel No Pain**.
- **Baffling Data Screed:** when a Tech-Priest's unit shoots (and isn't battle-shocked), roll a D6. On 2+, either an enemy vehicle within 12" takes a battle-shock roll at -1 (each vehicle only once per turn), or the unit **stays hidden** despite shooting.
- **Enhancements:**
  - *Vingh's Wafers of Dynamism* (25): Datasmith only. An attached unit gets Mobile for the whole game.
  - *TL-4Ø9* (30): any Tech-Priest. Extra gun: 24", 3 attacks, BS2+, S11, AP-2, D3+2, Devastating Wounds, Hazardous.
- **Stratagems (1CP each):**
  - *Scriptural Prognosis:* when an enemy targets a Tech-Priest unit that's on an objective, its attacks get -1 AP.
  - *Overloaded Safeguards:* a Tech-Priest unit can fall back and still shoot or charge.
  - *Holy Avarice:* a Tech-Priest unit can start an action and still shoot.

### Cohort Cybernetica (1 DP), the suggested third DP
- **Cyber-Psalm Programming:** LEGIO CYBERNETICA units (Kastelans, Datasmith) get +2" move and +1 OC unless battle-shocked.
- **Enhancements (Tech-Priest only):**
  - *Necromechanic* (20): once per battle round, turn the damage of one failed save on a friendly vehicle within 12" to 0.
  - *Lord of Machines* (15): in the enemy's Shooting phase, an enemy vehicle within 12" tests Leadership. On a pass it gets -1 to hit; on a fail it can't shoot.
  - *Emotionless Clarity* (10): a friendly vehicle within 12" that dies triggers its Deadly Demise automatically.
  - *Arch-negator* (5): the bearer's guns gain Anti-Vehicle 4+.
- **Stratagems (1CP each, all used in your Command phase on one AdMech vehicle or Cybernetica unit):**
  - *Transcendent Cogitation:* both Doctrinas at once. **The key one, for the Ironstrider.**
  - *Auto-divinatory Targeting:* guns hit on 3+ (2+ with Protector) and ignore cover, but can only shoot at units near one chosen objective.
  - *Motive Imperative:* +3" move and +1 to Advance and charge rolls.
  - *Machine Spirit Resurgent:* a damaged unit re-rolls hit rolls. If below half-strength, it also re-rolls wound rolls.
  - *Machine Superiority:* the unit can shoot after falling back and ignores modifiers to its rolls (except save modifiers).
  - *Benevolence of the Omnissiah:* 6+ Feel No Pain, improved to 5+ against mortal wounds.
