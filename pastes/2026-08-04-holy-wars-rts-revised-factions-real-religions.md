# Holy Wars: Smite First, Ask Questions Never
## Revised Faction Design — Real Religions, Real Doctrinal Differences

Tone: Equal-opportunity parody of religious *zealotry* and institutional behavior, not of sincere personal faith. Every faction is treated with the same loving mockery. The joke is always "people take this to absurd extremes" — the beliefs themselves are treated as real within the game world.

---

## Factions

---

### ✝️ The Holy Roman Catholic Church

**Theme:** Hierarchy, tradition, guilt, bureaucracy. The Vatican has issued a Papal Bull declaring this war necessary. Nobody has read it in full. The Pope is infallible about strategy (he is not good at strategy).

**Core Mechanic — Papal Authority:**
The Pope issues edicts periodically. You must comply. Edicts range from "attack the heretics to the north" (fine, that's what you were doing) to "observe a fast — no resource gathering Tuesday." Obeying grants large blessings. Defying costs you Excommunication points. Enough points and your units stop responding to orders until you do Penance (a 60-second idle animation).

**Mechanic — Saints Intercession:**
Research saints for specific passive bonuses. St. Sebastian = archers get +15% range. St. Michael = melee units deal holy damage. St. Anthony = you find 20% more relics (he helps find lost things). You can have 3 active saints at once and theologians argue about which ones count.

**Mechanic — Confession Cycle:**
Units accumulate Sin from killing enemies. High Sin = combat debuff (guilt). Units can visit a Confession building to reset Sin, gaining a brief absolution bonus. The Confession building has a queue. The queue is always long.

**Mechanic — Indulgences:**
Spend gold to skip the Confession queue, bypass Papal edicts, or purchase combat bonuses. Historically accurate. Generates a "Luther Tension" meter — max it out and a Reformation Schism event triggers.

**Super Ability — The Crusade:**
Declare a Holy War on any other faction. Your units gain +35% stats. Every other faction on the map gains a reason to dislike you. Stacks with previous Crusades.

**Hero — The Inquisitor:**
Visits enemy units and interrogates them. Units he visits either convert (10%) or become demoralized (90%). Nobody expects him.

### ☪️ The Caliphate

**Theme:** The Five Pillars as a living ruleset. A unified ummah with Shia/Sunni tension simmering underneath. Zakat as resource redistribution. Discipline and structure as mechanical strengths.

**Core Mechanic — The Five Pillars:**
Each Pillar is an active system:

1. **Shahada (Declaration):** Converting a neutral city permanently grants a Faith bonus. First conversion is free. The declaration rings out audibly across the map.
2. **Salat (Prayer):** Units pray 5 times per in-game day (each ~4 minutes). During prayer they're stationary but regenerate Faith rapidly. Attacks during prayer generate an *outrage bonus* for the rest of the day.
3. **Zakat (Charity):** 2.5% of your gold is redistributed to your weakest units automatically, buffing them. Disabling Zakat is technically possible. It makes your units very unhappy.
4. **Sawm (Fasting):** Activated Ramadan mode — units deal +20% damage, gather resources -30%. Month-long in-game events tied to this.
5. **Hajj (Pilgrimage):** Once per game, send a unit to the map's central holy site. If they arrive safely, you get the largest single blessing in the game. Other factions can intercept. They usually try.

**Mechanic — Shia/Sunni Tension:**
A hidden internal meter. Builds based on leadership decisions. At max, your army splits into two sub-factions that are still technically allied but have OPINIONS about each other's military decisions.

**Mechanic — Jihad vs. Ijtihad:**
Two research paths. Jihad = military bonuses, outward expansion, high-aggression units. Ijtihad = internal development, scholarship bonuses, science-rate resource multipliers. Most matches reward blending both, but purists exist.

**Hero — The Scholar-General:**
Buffs nearby units with tactical knowledge. Carries a translated copy of Greek philosophy. Gives adjacent Philosopher units (from the Greek faction, if allied) a mutual buff. Medieval accuracy.

### 🕉️ The Dharmic Coalition (Hinduism)

**Theme:** 330 million deities means 330 million possible intercessions. Karma is a real resource. The caste system is a tech tree. Reincarnation makes death complicated.

**Core Mechanic — Karma:**
Units accumulate Karma through combat actions. High Karma = better outcomes, crit rates, divine favor. Low Karma (from dishonorable actions — attacking fleeing units, betraying allies) = debuffs that persist across respawns. Yes, across respawns.

**Mechanic — Reincarnation:**
Units don't permanently die — they reincarnate after a delay. What they reincarnate *as* depends on their Karma. High Karma warriors come back as elite cavalry. Low Karma warriors come back as... something less useful. This means your army composition drifts based on how you've fought.

**Mechanic — The Pantheon Invocation:**
Invoke specific deities for specific effects. Not all deities agree on strategy:
- **Brahma:** Creates new units from nothing (long cooldown)
- **Vishnu:** Preserves and stabilizes — massive defensive buff to one location
- **Shiva:** Destroys — massive AoE damage, including some friendly units. Shiva does not discriminate.
- **Lakshmi:** Doubles resource income for 2 minutes
- **Kali:** Sends your most veteran unit into a berserker state. She will not stop.
- **Ganesh:** Removes all obstacles from a path — enemy fortifications on a route just... open.

**Mechanic — Dharma Path:**
Each unit has a Dharma (warrior, merchant, priest, worker). Using a unit for its Dharma role gives bonuses. Using them outside it gives penalties. A merchant unit CAN fight. They're bad at it and feel weird about it.

**Super Ability — The Mahabharata Invocation:**
Krishna personally drives your hero unit's chariot. Hero becomes temporarily unkillable. He also lectures them on duty and impermanence the entire time. Enemies near the chariot flee from the discourse.

**Hero — The Rishi:**
Sits down. Meditates. Generates enormous passive Faith for the entire army. Cannot be ordered to move without breaking the meditation. Other factions' heroes challenge him to debates. He wins all of them. This has no gameplay effect.

### ✡️ The Covenant (Judaism)

**Theme:** Chosen People mechanics, Talmudic argumentation as a tech tree, the defensive genius of a people who have survived everything, and 613 commandments as both buffs and constraints.

**Core Mechanic — The 613 Mitzvot:**
613 commandments are active rules. Most are minor passive effects. Some matter enormously:
- **No work on Shabbat:** Saturday (one in-game day per week) your economy halts. Your military can still operate. Your economy will not. Plan ahead.
- **Kashrut (Dietary laws):** Units cannot be in the same formation as certain other unit types. This affects army composition in subtle ways.
- **Pikuach Nefesh:** If a unit's life is at risk, all other laws can be suspended. This is the emergency override — breaks Shabbat, breaks formation rules, breaks everything if you're about to be wiped.

**Mechanic — Talmudic Debate:**
Two rabbis argue about every strategic decision before it's implemented. The debate generates a ruling. The ruling is correct. It takes 45 seconds. Enemy attacks during this period hit a "we're discussing it" buff that reduces damage by 15%.

**Mechanic — Diaspora Network:**
Units can be stationed "abroad" in neutral territory without declaring war. They generate passive income and intelligence. They cannot fight without triggering a formal conflict. They are everywhere.

**Mechanic — The Covenant Bonus:**
Every unit has a baseline stat bonus for being "chosen." The bonus is modest. It is always there. It cannot be removed by enemy debuffs. Other factions find this statistically irritating.

**Super Ability — Exodus:**
Your entire army phases through any terrain, walls, or obstacles for 90 seconds. They're leaving. You can direct where they emerge. The Red Sea is optional.

**Hero — The Golem:**
Enormous. Made of clay. No Faith meter (golems don't have souls, this is theologically precise). Cannot convert. Cannot be demoralized. Responds only to the name written on its forehead. Changing the name changes its orders. Accidentally erasing it is a self-goal.

### ☸️ The Sangha (Buddhism)

**Theme:** Non-violence creates enormous mechanical tension in a war game. Enlightenment is a win condition. Monks are support powerhouses. The Middle Path makes extremes costly.

**Core Mechanic — The Ahimsa Constraint:**
Killing enemy units generates Suffering — a debuff resource. High Suffering slows your entire army spiritually (reduced Faith, reduced miracle potency). You can fight. Killing is just expensive. This creates constant pressure toward conversion and routing over slaughter.

**Mechanic — The Middle Path:**
Extremes in any direction are penalized. Spending too much gold = debuff. Spending too little = debuff. Maximum aggression = Suffering spike. Maximum passivity = vulnerability. The game rewards balance. Players who optimize aggressively find themselves confused about why everything is slightly wrong.

**Mechanic — Enlightenment:**
Units can achieve Enlightenment through sustained non-violent action (converting enemies, healing allies, holding holy sites peacefully). Enlightened units transcend combat — they become support entities that buff ALL nearby units regardless of faction. Including enemies. This is correct behavior and is not a bug.

**Mechanic — Karma Wheel:**
What you do returns to you. Aggressive expansions trigger enemy coalitions. Peaceful development triggers voluntary city alliances. The map literally rewards being decent.

**Super Ability — Dharma Wheel Turn:**
A pulse goes out from your main temple. All enemy units in range must pass a Faith check or become "uncertain about the purpose of this conflict" for 30 seconds, during which they will not attack.

**Win Condition — Nirvana:**
Unique alternate win: achieve Enlightenment with 25 units without those units ever directly killing an enemy. The game acknowledges this. A special screen appears. It is very calm.

**Hero — The Bodhisattva:**
Chose to remain in the conflict to help others achieve enlightenment. Could have won already. Is choosing not to, as a gift. Has the highest support stats in the game. Will not explain why they seem so untroubled.

### ⛪ The Reformation Assembly (Protestantism)

**Theme:** 40,000 denominations and climbing. The Pope has no authority here. Every man reads the Bible himself and reaches different conclusions. Schism is not a crisis — it is a feature.

**Core Mechanic — Sola Scriptura (Bible Alone):**
No Pope. No Saints bonuses. Just the text. Research unlocks Biblical interpretations — each one buffs a unit type. Different interpretations sometimes contradict. When two contradictory interpretations are both active, a Denominational Dispute event fires. You now have two slightly different Protestant factions fighting under your banner. They cooperate. Mostly.

**Mechanic — The Denominational Tree:**
Your faction starts as "Generic Protestant." Research splits you:
- **Lutheran branch:** Strong on grace, weak on church hierarchy, bonus to faith-based abilities
- **Calvinist branch:** Predestination mechanic — elite units are pre-selected at game start and will survive disproportionately (they were chosen)
- **Anglican branch:** Mostly Catholic with different leadership. Surprising nobody.
- **Baptist branch:** Emphasis on individual conversion. Missionaries are twice as effective.
- **Evangelical branch:** Enormous rallies. Giant AoE morale events. Tent revivals as mobile Faith generators.
- **Megachurch branch:** See previous design. Gold everywhere.

**Mechanic — The Reformation Thesis:**
Post theses on enemy faction buildings. Each thesis posted generates a public debate event. Win the debate (RNG + your Scholar units' stats) and the building's loyalty shifts. Lose and the debate is used as enemy propaganda.

**Mechanic — The Work Ethic:**
Protestant work ethic is a real passive. Resource generation is +20% at all times. Units feel slightly guilty if they're idle. Idle units self-assign tasks.

**Super Ability — Great Awakening:**
A revival sweeps your forces. Every unit's Faith is instantly maxed. They are very excited. For 45 seconds they will not stop.

**Hero — The Reformer:**
Carries a hammer. Has strong opinions about doors. When he speaks, enemy units in range must make a Faith saving throw or start questioning their current faction's doctrine. Some of them come back later as converts. Some of them start a new denomination. This is beyond your control.

## Doctrinal Disputes as Live Events

Every 10 minutes, a random Doctrinal Dispute event fires. Both factions involved must "respond" (spend Scholar units on a debate). Examples:

- **"Transubstantiation vs. Consubstantiation"** — Catholics vs. Lutherans. Stakes: control of all bakeries on the map (bread matters)
- **"Predestination vs. Free Will"** — Calvinists vs. Catholics. The Calvinist player argues they were always going to win this debate.
- **"Iconoclasm Incident"** — Any faction with Relic bonuses must defend them from an iconoclast event
- **"Which Direction to Face During Prayer"** — Caliphate internal event. Minor. Intense.
- **"Is Suffering Inherent or Caused by Attachment"** — Buddhist vs. Hindu philosophical standoff. Both sides buff while debating. Nobody wins. This is the point.
- **"What Happened at the Council of Nicaea"** — Fires randomly. Nobody fully remembers. Everyone has opinions.

## Revised Win Conditions

- **Territorial Dominance:** Control 70% of holy sites (now including: the Holy Sepulchre, the Western Wall, the Kaaba, Bodh Gaya, the Golden Temple, Mecca adjacency zone, Rome)
- **Conversion Dominance:** 60% of all units share your faith
- **The Reformation Win:** (Protestant only) Post enough Theses that 3 other factions have internal Schism events simultaneously
- **Nirvana Win:** (Buddhist only) 25 Enlightened units, zero direct kills
- **Exodus Win:** (Jewish only) Survive every other faction declaring war on you simultaneously for 10 minutes. You've done it before.
- **Dharmachakra Win:** (Hindu only) Have all 6 major deities invoked in the same 60-second window
