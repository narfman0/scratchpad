**Adeptus Mechanicus — Lords of the Forge** · Strike Force · 1,980 pts · Warlord: Belisarius Cawl · 11th ed · [New Recruit list](https://www.newrecruit.eu/app/Lists/6ac655411c05a91bd0553bde?view=main)

> **Game plan:** Hold the middle with a tough group of units around Cawl. A shooting line covers them from behind, and cheap fast units score and screen. Take objectives, let the enemy come to you, and hit back with the Kastelans and Breachers.

## Detachment: War-Form Mantles

- All **Tech-Priests**: **4+ invulnerable save, 5+ Feel No Pain**.
- **Baffling Data Screed** (Tech-Priest's unit, when it shoots, not battle-shocked, roll 2+):
  - an enemy VEHICLE within 12" takes a battle-shock test at −1, *or*
  - this unit's shooting doesn't stop it being hidden.

### Stratagems (1CP)

- **Scriptural Prognosis:** Tech-Priest unit near an objective is targeted → attacks against it get −1 AP.
- **Overloaded Safeguards:** that move doesn't stop the unit shooting or charging.
- **Holy Avarice:** that action doesn't stop the unit shooting.

## Doctrina Imperatives (pick each battle round)

**PROTECTOR** — +1 BS, ranged weapons get [HEAVY]. Battleline units, and units within 6" of Battleline: melee attacks against them get **−1 to hit**.
*Use on turn 1, when shooting from cover, and when you're about to be charged.*

**CONQUEROR** — +1 WS, ranged weapons get [ASSAULT]. Battleline units, and units within 6" of Battleline: **+1 AP** on all their attacks.
*Use on the turns you attack. Cawl counts as Battleline!*

## Turn by turn

- **Deploy:** middle group in the centre behind cover, with the Vanguard and Dominus in the Dunerider. Push the Infiltrators forward, keep the Pteraxii in reserve, and give the shooters firing lanes.
- **T1 · Protector:** shoot from safe spots, take the easy objectives, screen. Keep the hammers out of sight.
- **T2 · Conqueror:** push the middle group, drop the Vanguard from the Dunerider, send in the Kastelans and Breachers, bring in the Pteraxii.
- **T3+:** trade units on the middle objectives. Use Cawl's +1 OC to win close objective fights. Fast units go after secondaries. Swap Doctrina based on whether you're attacking or defending.

## Remember

- **Cawl powers the whole middle.** Never expose him.
- **Conqueror + Cawl within 6"** = +1 AP on everything nearby. That's your big damage turn.
- **Against tanks:** neutron Onager, TL-4Ø9, Breachers and Cawl all shoot the same target.
- **Against melee armies:** stay in Protector near Battleline units for −1 to hit, then hit back with the Kastelans and Breachers.
- **Command phase:** pick the Kastelan protocol, and decide whether to use the Manipulus 4+ invulnerable save or the Marshal's battle-shock removal.
- **Fast units are expendable.** Use them to give up a little ground and block moves.
